# Z4SK — sellable asset register

This file distinguishes assets already implemented from legal protections that still require registration or signed paperwork. Software features alone do not prevent copying; the defensible package is the combination of brand, proprietary data, distribution loops, operational know-how and clean ownership.

## Assets implemented in this repository

1. **Product and source code.** The complete Z4SK web/PWA, private response workflow, destruction mechanics, double-blind match logic, multilingual UI and founder dashboard.
2. **Question Library v2026.08.** A structured five-language content library with intensity levels. Keep dated source files and editorial change logs as evidence of authorship.
3. **Smart Mix™.** A privacy-safe ranking system that learns from selection, completion and answer-length signals without analyzing answer text.
4. **Country trend dataset.** Aggregate country, locale and question-performance tables with a minimum publication threshold. The growing dataset—not an individual answer—is the strategic asset.
5. **Viral distribution funnel.** Event taxonomy covering create, open, respond, match, unlock, report, destroy and delete events, with source and sponsor-pack attribution.
6. **Sponsor Pack system.** Theme and attribution architecture for brands, venues and creators.
7. **Reusable SDK.** `packages/z4sk-sdk` packages the creation and embed workflow for licensing as “Viral Quiz as a Service.”
8. **Safety and privacy operations.** Short-lived content, secret bearer dashboards, view-once deletion, aggregate-only analytics, reporting and rate limiting.

## Protection actions before a sale

- Run a trademark clearance search, then apply to register **Z4SK** and the final logo in target markets. The repository does not claim that a trademark is registered.
- Secure the primary domain and defensive spelling variants in the company’s name.
- Make every founder, employee and contractor sign invention-assignment and confidentiality agreements covering code, designs, translations and question copy.
- Add a commercial software license and copyright notices after the owning legal entity is selected. Keep the repository private until the launch strategy says otherwise.
- Record third-party dependency licenses and retain invoices/terms for commissioned visual assets and translations.
- Register relevant copyright deposits where commercially useful; obtain local counsel before claiming database rights or trade-secret protection.
- Document the Smart Mix parameters, moderation thresholds and growth playbook in a restricted trade-secret appendix, not in public marketing materials.

## Acquisition data room

- chain-of-title agreements and contributor list
- trademark/domain records and brand guidelines
- clean source archive, release tags, dependency/license report and security audit
- anonymized cohort metrics: create→open→respond→remix, retention, match rate and sponsor conversion
- data dictionary, retention schedule, deletion evidence and incident log
- customer/sponsor contracts, pricing experiments and SDK integration guide
- monthly P&L, infrastructure costs and a credentials/operations handover checklist

## Recommended moat sequence

First grow the unique aggregate dataset and remix loop. Next sign exclusive sponsor or influencer packs. Then license the SDK to third parties. Brand registration and clean IP ownership make those commercial advantages transferable to a buyer.
